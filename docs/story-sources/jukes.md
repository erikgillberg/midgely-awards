# Sources: jukes (Thomas H. Jukes and Robert Stokstad, antibiotics as growth promoters in feed)

Draft: `carousel.json` in this folder. Pattern: resistance (secondary: everyone, moved). Foresight: knowable (record `knowable: knowable`; see notes.md).

Key sources, abbreviated below:
- **PSEBM 1950**: Stokstad and Jukes, "Further Observations on the 'Animal Protein Factor,'" *Proc. Soc. Exp. Biol. Med.* 73:523 to 528 (1950), received February 10, 1950. Byline: Lederle Laboratories Division, American Cyanamid Company, Pearl River, NY. doi:10.3181/00379727-73-17731. Read in full (PDF from ebm-journal.org).
- **McKenna 2017**: Maryn McKenna, "The Chicken Experiment That Shook the World," *Undark*, October 6, 2017, excerpted from *Big Chicken* (National Geographic, 2017). https://undark.org/article/chicken-experiment-shook-world/
- **Choices 2015**: Teillant and Laxminarayan, "Economics of Antibiotic Use in U.S. Swine and Poultry Production," *Choices* 30(1), 1st Quarter 2015. https://www.choicesmagazine.org/UserFiles/file/cmsarticle_404.pdf
- **Levy 1976**: Levy, FitzGerald and Macone, "Changes in intestinal flora of farm personnel after introduction of a tetracycline-supplemented feed on a farm," *NEJM* 295(11):583 to 588 (1976). https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/950974
- **FDA 2016 report**: FDA, *2016 Summary Report on Antimicrobials Sold or Distributed for Use in Food-Producing Animals* (December 2017). Copy: https://www.cdfa.ca.gov/ahfss/AUS/docs/FDA_2016_Summary_Report_Antimicrobials_Sold.pdf
- **FDA 2024 data**: FDA, 2024 Summary Report data tables, 2015 to 2024 (Excel). https://www.fda.gov/media/189753/download?attachment ; announcement: https://www.fda.gov/animal-veterinary/cvm-updates/fda-releases-annual-summary-sales-and-distribution-antimicrobials-2024-use-food-producing-animals
- **ESVAC 2022**: EMA, *Sales of veterinary antimicrobial agents in 31 European countries in 2022* (Thirteenth ESVAC report, 2023). https://www.ema.europa.eu/en/documents/report/sales-veterinary-antimicrobial-agents-31-european-countries-2022-trends-2010-2022-thirteenth-esvac-report_en.pdf
- **Aarestrup 2001**: Aarestrup and others, *Antimicrob. Agents Chemother.* 45(7):2054 to 2059 (2001). https://doi.org/10.1128/AAC.45.7.2054-2059.2001 (abstract read via NCBI efetch)
- **Liu 2016**: Liu and others, "Emergence of plasmid-mediated colistin resistance mechanism MCR-1 in animals and human beings in China," *Lancet Infect. Dis.* 16(2):161 to 168 (2016; online November 19, 2015). https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/26603172
- **OECD 2015**: Laxminarayan, Van Boeckel and Teillant, *The Economic Costs of Withdrawing Anti-microbial Growth Promoters from the Livestock Sector*, OECD Food, Agriculture and Fisheries Papers No. 78 (2015). https://onehealthtrust.org/wp-content/uploads/2017/06/5js64kst5wvl.pdf
- **FR 2011**: FDA, withdrawal of 1977 notices of opportunity for hearing, *Federal Register* 76(246):79697, December 22, 2011. https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/FR-2011-12-22/html/2011-32775.htm
