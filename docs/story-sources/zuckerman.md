## Judgment calls

1. **What he invented.** The draft never says he invented pop-up windows or JavaScript windows. It says he wrote the code that opened Tripod's ad in its own window, which he calls "probably the first popup." Wikipedia notes he claims only to have written the code, while he is credited as the inventor.
2. **Pattern: everyone, with moved second.** Each site gained from pop-ups; the flood spent readers' shared patience until browsers turned pop-ups off for everyone, good sites and bad. "Moved" covers the shift of the advertiser's problem onto readers. Rejected: gamed, metric.
3. **The "original sin" claim is his argument, not a documented effect.** The surveillance-based ad model is his 2014 thesis about advertising as a default, not something the pop-up caused. The draft attributes it to him and keeps it off the backfire card's harm list; Pew's 72% appears only in risks and events as context.
4. **Ad blocking is not all about pop-ups.** Ad-blocking counts and the $21.8 billion figure cover intrusive ads of every kind. Cards and charts say so ("ads of every kind"). The direct, documented pop-up backfire is the browser blockers of 2002 to 2004.
5. **Image of the Atlantic's art left out.** The Atlantic illustrated the essay with Goltzius, "The Fall of Man" (1616, CC0 on Commons), but the painting is full nudity, so it was dropped for Instagram. An in-page pop-up screenshot on Commons was dropped because it is under the Mozilla Public License, not CC or public domain.
6. **The page "about sex".** His wording is "a page that celebrated anal sex." The card says "a member's page about sex" to keep the scope true without the detail.
7. **Cover.** Candidates: "Pop-ups began as a fix for an upset advertiser" (46, chosen); "The pop-up ad was built to protect advertisers" (46, runner-up for A/B); "The pop-up ad's coder said sorry in 2014" (39, gives away the ending); "Pop-up ads were invented to keep ads tasteful" (45, stretches his words); "Pop-ups: a fix for one ad, a cost for all" (41, gives away the pattern).
8. **Hindsight item 3** (sell ads by section, as print did) rests on our inference that Tripod's interest pods could have carried brand-sensitive ads; marked unverified in risks.json.
