## Judgment calls
1. **Franz is not living.** The National Inventors Hall of Fame lists "Born Dec. 21, 1929 - Died April 27, 2026." The nominee record still says "Born 1929," so the record's `years` field needs an update (I did not edit nominees.json). The cards still follow the living-person rules: only public decisions, nothing about intent, and the health question attributed to the agencies.
2. **Health is presented as contested,** with both sides named (IARC hazard classification against EPA and EFSA risk conclusions). Card text does not say glyphosate causes cancer. The settlement line says "no admission of liability" and quotes Bayer's stated reason for the residential change.
3. **Not on the cards, for space or fairness:**
   - The June 25, 2026 Supreme Court ruling (Monsanto v. Durnell, 7 to 2, federal label law preempts a state failure-to-warn claim; it did not decide whether glyphosate causes cancer).
   - The $7.25 billion class settlement proposed in February 2026, with final approval pending as of early October 2026.
   - The Ninth Circuit's 2022 vacatur of the human health part of EPA's 2020 interim decision.
   - EPA's updated human health assessment, which its page says is expected in late 2026.
   - EU renewal to December 15, 2033.

   Any of these could replace a line on the cleanup card. The cleanup card is labeled "Where it stands" because nothing has been undone yet.
4. **Agency names.** The foresight card says "a WHO agency" (IARC) and "US and EU regulators" (EPA, EFSA, ECHA). EPA's 2017 conclusion stands on its page, but the court vacated the 2020 decision that repeated it, and EPA is re-explaining its cancer evaluation. A reviewer may want "US and EU regulators have concluded."
5. **Bradshaw 1997.** The authors were Monsanto employees. The card says "Monsanto scientists" and describes the paper's finding about the record to date. It does not say they predicted resistance would never happen, which is how some critics characterize it.
6. **Pattern.** `resistance` is a direct fit. The mirror (spam word filters selecting for misspellings) works by selecting for survivors, the same mechanism.
7. **Benbrook** is an estimate. The author has worked with organic-sector groups. The 15-fold figure covers global use from 1996 to 2014.
8. **Credit.** Franz said the discovery was a team effort (per Wikipedia, not verified in a primary source). Not on the cards.
