Nominations must be about public work

Nominate decisions and their effects, not people's private lives. Only nominate people for public work, such as inventions, products, policies and plans. Private individuals cannot be nominated. Leave out claims about a person's motives, character or private life. Stick to effects that are documented, and mark debated evidence as "Still contested."
