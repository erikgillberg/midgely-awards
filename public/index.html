The Backfire Awards

For outstanding achievement in unintended consequences.

The best fixes often cause the next problem. Leaded gasoline made engines run better and put lead in children's blood. We collect backfires like it, score them and argue about them, so the next big fix gets a second look.

No. 1MgMIDGLEY

The Midgley

Thomas Midgley Jr. invented leaded gasoline and Freon. Both solved real problems, and the world spent decades cleaning up after them.

For backfires at world scale.

Anyone can nominate one. Judges give it an Oops Score, readers give it a Herd Score, and the winners are named each year.

What makes a good nominee · 2 of 3

It caused harm somewhere else

The damage landed on people or places the inventors weren't thinking about. Leaded gasoline made engines run better, and the lead ended up in children's blood.

What makes a good nominee · 3 of 3

Say what people knew

Could anyone have seen the harm coming at the time? Midgley's lead hazard was known. The damage Freon did to the ozone layer wasn't understood until 1974.
