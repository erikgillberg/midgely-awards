The element tile

One symbol, four numbers and one word. Every number is a fixed fact, so a tile saved as an image never goes out of date. Scores and rankings change, so they live on the website only.

The periodic table of backfires

Each category is a column, colored by its jewel. A new nominee joins the bottom of its column and takes the next number, so the table can grow to hundreds.

Image sources

Every image on a carousel has a provenance record in the database: where it came from, who made it, the license, what we changed, and when we took it.
